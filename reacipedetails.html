Chicken Biryani

Category: Pakistani

Cooking Time: 45 Minutes

Author: RecipeShare User

Date: 20 September 2026

Description

Delicious and flavorful Pakistani chicken biryani made with aromatic spices and rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Rice
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Biryani Masala
  • Yogurt

Instructions

Cook the chicken with spices. Prepare the rice. Mix the rice and chicken together and cook until everything is fully prepared.
